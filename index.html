A developer who cares about the last 10% of polish.

I'm Olosunde Glory Oluwaferanmi, a frontend developer from Nigeria. I build responsive, clean, and interactive websites and dashboards — the kind that hold up on a slow connection and a small screen, not just on a designer's monitor.

I like the problem-solving side of code as much as the visual side: getting a layout to behave, a form to feel effortless, or a dashboard to load fast. Outside of pure frontend work, I'm building out my Python skills for the logic that sits behind the interface.
